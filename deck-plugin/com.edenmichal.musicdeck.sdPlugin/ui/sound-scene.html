Press to fade to this scene: its layers in, every other scene's out. Hold for half a second to add it on top instead, so several scenes play at once; hold it again to fade just this scene out. The key lights while its scene plays. Make and edit scenes in Foundry's Ambient tab. To change lights and sound together, put this action in a Multi Action after the Philips Hue “Set Scene” (a Multi Action always switches). To play several scenes from one key, use a Scene Combo key instead.
