Tracks come from Foundry's Library: your playlists, then the folders in Data/music/. Press: the deck that's playing fades from its track to this one (it joins that deck's queue, right after what was playing). If nothing is playing, the track starts on the deck you're hearing. During a battle it plays on the Battle deck instead, and A and B stay paused. The crossfader doesn't move. The key lights while this track plays.
