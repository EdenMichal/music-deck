Type a pad number (1–8, the numbers in Foundry's track editor) or a cue name. A name works on every track that has a cue called that, so one “Phase 2” key does every boss. Press: switch at the end of the loop (or as set in Foundry). Hold: switch now, with a short crossfade. The key lights while you hear its cue and blinks while the switch waits. “Cue” is ignored for Next and Previous.
